[fix] Text a document shows after integrating an update across a missing one is no longer dropped when its bytes are loaded again. With @y/y 14.0.0-rc.29, a struct that arrives on its own is integrated past an earlier, blocked struct of its client, but `encodeStateAsUpdate` batches them, and a plain decode held the later struct (and anything typed after it) pending. `createYDoc(stored)`, the headless helpers, `migrateYState`, `prepareYRoom`, `sourceView` and `createYQuarantineRecord` now integrate every struct whose dependencies are present, as the encoding document did; a state with nothing pending still loads in one apply. A loaded document can also show a struct the encoding document still held pending (the bytes cannot tell the two apart), placed where it lands once the missing updates arrive; a format opener whose closer is still blocked then runs to the end of the paragraph until the gap heals. Live sync between peers (sync step 2, applied with `Y.applyUpdate`) is unchanged. The repair makes at most 1,024 suffix applies in one load (one can integrate many structs, so only pathological input reaches the budget); past it the rest stays pending, never lost, and integrates when its missing updates arrive.

Verify: let a client integrate an update past a missing earlier one of the same writer (the relay drops one update and batches the next two), type after the integrated text, and load `encodeStateAsUpdate` of that client with `createYDoc`: the document shows the text and the typing. Once the missing update arrives, the loaded document and a plain `Y.applyUpdate` load end byte-identical.
